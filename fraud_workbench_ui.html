📊 Base Risk (SHAP Expected Value)
This is the model's baseline prediction - the average fraud score across all providers in the training data.
How to interpret:
  • • This is the "starting point" before considering this provider's specific features
  • • SHAP values then adjust UP or DOWN from this baseline
  • • Final Score = Base Risk + Sum of all SHAP values
💡 A base risk of ~0.83 means the average provider has an 83% fraud probability in the model's view
